If this project is conducted in India, the responsible research institution must determine which requirements apply. Potentially relevant areas include institutional ethics review, informed-consent requirements, applicable Indian health/research regulations, applicable personal-data protection requirements, institutional retention requirements, and applicable CDSCO requirements if the eventual intended use falls within regulated medical-device or software scope.
