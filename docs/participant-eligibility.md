# Participant eligibility framework

**All criteria below are PROPOSED — REQUIRES STUDY APPROVAL.** They are configurable in the approved protocol; this repository does not establish clinically validated eligibility rules.

## Potential inclusion variables

- **PROPOSED — REQUIRES STUDY APPROVAL:** adult participants, if the approved protocol specifies adults.
- **PROPOSED — REQUIRES STUDY APPROVAL:** ability to provide informed consent.
- **PROPOSED — REQUIRES STUDY APPROVAL:** availability of an approved laboratory Hb measurement.
- **PROPOSED — REQUIRES STUDY APPROVAL:** ability to undergo smartphone image capture.

## Potential exclusion variables

- **PROPOSED — REQUIRES STUDY APPROVAL:** inability to consent.
- **PROPOSED — REQUIRES STUDY APPROVAL:** ocular conditions that interfere with visualization under the approved protocol.
- **PROPOSED — REQUIRES STUDY APPROVAL:** severe image-acquisition obstruction.
- **PROPOSED — REQUIRES STUDY APPROVAL:** missing approved laboratory reference.
- **PROPOSED — REQUIRES STUDY APPROVAL:** protocol violation affecting data interpretability.

The responsible research team must version the final criteria, train operators on them, and document screening decisions without placing direct identifiers in the research dataset.
